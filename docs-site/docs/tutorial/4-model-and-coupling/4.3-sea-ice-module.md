# 4.3 Thermodynamic Sea Ice

This chapter describes the thermodynamic sea-ice kernel of PlaSiC: it updates the ice/snow surface temperature, the snow amount, and the ice thickness once per coupling step. The atmospheric fluxes it consumes are accumulated over the coupling window by `sea_step()`, as described in [4.1 How the Spheres Are Coupled](4.1-how-the-spheres-are-coupled.md), and the mixed-layer ocean it exchanges heat with is updated by the slab ocean in [4.4 Slab Ocean](4.4-slab-ocean.md).

The ice kernels implement a zero-layer thermodynamic model with one effective surface heat capacity. They do not predict ice motion, horizontal ice transport, ridging, or internal stress. The update order in `physics_processes.c` is shown in the flow chart below.
